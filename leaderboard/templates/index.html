* Partial. The benchmark score always divides by all {{ meta.scoreable_workloads }} scoreable workloads, so a workload that was never attempted counts as zero exactly like one that failed. A starred row was not run on the whole benchmark, and its headline is a floor, not a measurement of what that submission can do. No row is unstarred today — even the eager-PyTorch sweep is missing a handful of workloads — so read the star as "how much is missing", in the two counts under mean (attempted), rather than as a pass/fail mark.

Reference variants are not agent results. The four PyTorch formulations are what Tb is derived from — Tb is the fastest of them per workload — so whichever one wins a workload scores exactly 0.5 there by construction. They calibrate the scale; they do not compete on it.

Every number here is {{ meta.part or 'this part' }}, at FLOCK {{ meta.f_lock_mhz }} MHz. TSOL in milliseconds, Tb and FLOCK all differ between parts, so a score measured on one cannot be ranked against a score measured on another — the scorer refuses that comparison rather than rescaling it. The other part this port targets, MI355X, is in the switch above with nothing behind it: nothing has been measured there, so it has no FLOCK, and without FLOCK no TSOL in milliseconds, no Tb and no S.
