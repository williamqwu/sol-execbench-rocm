A starred row was not run on the whole benchmark, and its headline is a floor, not a measurement of what that submission can do. No row is unstarred today — even the eager-PyTorch sweep is missing a handful of workloads — so read the star as "how much is missing", in the two counts under mean (attempted), rather than as a pass/fail mark.

Reference variants are not agent results. The four PyTorch formulations are what Tb is derived from — Tb is the fastest of them per workload — so whichever one wins a workload scores exactly 0.5 there by construction. They calibrate the scale; they do not compete on it.

Every number here is {{ meta.part or 'this part' }}, at FLOCK {{ meta.f_lock_mhz }} MHz. TSOL in milliseconds, Tb and FLOCK all differ between parts, so a score measured on one cannot be ranked against a score measured on another — the scorer refuses that comparison rather than rescaling it. A second port (MI355X, FLOCK 1640) is in progress on feat/agent-scoreboard and is not shown: its Tb is not yet anchor-verified, so it has no S to publish.
