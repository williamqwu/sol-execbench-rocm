Workloads, and the two bounds each is scored between

Baseline Tb is the fastest of four optimized-PyTorch formulations, measured on this part — a kernel that ties it scores exactly 0.5. SOL TSOL is the physical roofline, derived on AMD and converted at FLOCK {{ meta.f_lock_mhz }} MHz — a kernel that reaches it scores 1.0, and nothing may beat it. Headroom is the ratio between them: how much of the score is really a distance from the roofline rather than a comparison with PyTorch. How a bound is derived →

How TSOL and Tb were arrived at, per workload. TSOL (cyc) is the bound as it is actually derived — in GPU cycles, which is architectural and so does not move with the clock; the millisecond column is this divided by FLOCK. Bound source names which of two independent derivations won: the SOLAR analytic model, or declared traffic over the roofline. They are combined with max, and any candidate that came out faster than the measured Tb was rejected rather than used. Bottleneck is which side of the roofline the workload sits on, bandwidth or arithmetic. Tb variant is which of the four PyTorch formulations was fastest here and therefore set the anchor. atol and rtol are not performance at all: they are the numeric tolerance a submission's output must match the reference within, re-derived on AMD from run-to-run error rather than carried over.
